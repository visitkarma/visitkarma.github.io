## About Visit Karma

We're a city-dwelling family with a love for luxury travel and outdoor adventures — and **Visit Karma** is where we share what we actually experience on the road.

From ski-in/ski-out hotels in the Alps to overwater villas in the Maldives and all-inclusive resorts in the Caribbean, every review here is based on a stay we took ourselves. We write the honest, detailed guides we wish we'd had before booking: what the rooms are really like, whether the dining lives up to the price, how family-friendly a property is, and whether it's worth the splurge.

## How we travel — and how we don't

Nobody pays us to travel. We don't take sponsored stays, free upgrades in exchange for coverage, or 'influencer' discounts — and we've never been paid to review a hotel, resort, or airline. Before we book anything, we spend a lot of time researching — comparing options, reading the fine print, checking what past guests actually experienced — so in most cases what we pick is already the highest-percentage experience in that location: the stay most likely to be great, not just the most advertised. Then we go, we pay our own way, and we write what actually happened, including the bad bits. The only bias we have is wanting our family to enjoy the vacation — same as you.

- **Family travel planning** — getting the most out of trips without overspending
